date = 2026-11-28

ParticleScope collects no personal data.

- No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.
- The app runs entirely on your device. All simulations and pictures are part of it; it never sends anything you do to anyone.
- It stores nothing on your device: no cookies, no saved data. The browser version only loads its own files from this website.
- The browser version is served by Cloudflare, the host of this website. Like any web server, it handles technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages; see [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

The same applies to the iOS, Android and Steam versions.

Questions: see the contact details on the [About](/about/) page.

Last updated: 28 November 2026.
