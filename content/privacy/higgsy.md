+++
title = 'Higgsy: privacy policy'
date = 2026-11-28
+++

Higgsy collects no personal data.

- No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases.
- The game runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests. It never sends anything you do to anyone.
- Your game progress and settings (such as the language) are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.
- If you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your result and hands it to your phone's share menu. Where it goes is your choice; Higgsy itself sends nothing.

This applies to the Android and iOS versions.

Questions: see the contact details on the [About](/about/) page.
