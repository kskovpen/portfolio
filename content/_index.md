I am a particle physicist at Ghent University, working on the CMS experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider. My research focuses on the top quark, the heaviest known elementary particle: searches for flavour-changing neutral currents, effective-field-theory interpretations, and the observation of four-top-quark production. I also work on analysis software and computing for high-energy physics.
