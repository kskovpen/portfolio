I'm a physicist at Ghent University in Belgium. I work with data from the Large Hadron Collider at CERN, the world's largest particle accelerator, where I study the top quark, the heaviest elementary particle we know of. In 2023 our experiment observed four top quarks being produced at once, the heaviest combination of particles ever seen at the collider.

Outside work I run, mostly marathons and trail races around Brussels, and I like building electronics and writing software.
