ParticleScope shows particles the way physicists have watched them for a century: as droplets in a cloud chamber, bubbles in liquid hydrogen, sparks, flashes of light and lit-up silicon pixels.

Every scene is simulated with the software physicists use at CERN (Geant4 and Garfield++) and checked against the original measurements, from Rutherford’s gold foil to Super-Kamiokande. Twelve experiments, each with a short story and a guide for teachers.

Free, with no ads and no data collected. App Store, Google Play and Steam versions are on the way.
