The digital potentiometers carry a significant amount of parasitic capacitance,
which can reach up to 100 pF, severely affecting the performance of
the operational amplifier, when introduced in its feedback.
Calibration of op amp with placing a small capacitor on one of its
inputs introduces an important impedance affecting its performance.
