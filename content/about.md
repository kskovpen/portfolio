I'm a particle physicist at [Ghent University](https://www.ugent.be) in Belgium. I study the smallest building blocks of matter with the Large Hadron Collider at CERN near Geneva, and help build the detectors that photograph what happens when protons collide. [My research]({{< relref "/science" >}}) centres on the top quark, the heaviest particle we know: in 2023 I was one of the main people behind the [first observation of four top quarks produced at once]({{< relref "/science/cms/top-quark/four-tops" >}}).

I studied and did my doctorate in Novosibirsk, then worked in Strasbourg and Brussels before settling in Ghent. I [teach and give talks]({{< relref "/talks" >}}), make [games]({{< relref "/projects" >}}) that explain physics to everyone, [run]({{< relref "/run" >}}) trails and marathons, and [make music]({{< relref "/music" >}}).
